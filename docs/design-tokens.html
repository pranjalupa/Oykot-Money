Five colors, three scales

Dark Green and the pale "Gray" both read as green — so they anchor one Forest scale together, with Gray as its lightest tint rather than a neutral. White and Black have no hue at all, so they anchor a true neutral Stone scale instead — that's what backgrounds, borders and dark mode are built from now, which is why dark mode no longer reads as a dark green room. Lemon Yellow stays its own accent. Steps marked given are your exact hex values.

What each color is for

Named the way shadcn/ui expects them, so this maps straight into globals.css later. Background, card, border and muted all come from Stone now, in both themes — Forest and Lemon are reserved for actions and emphasis, never the base surface. Primary swaps from Forest to Lemon in dark mode, since dark green loses contrast on a near-black ground.

Archivo, Inter, Instrument Serif

Archivo carries headings and the big numbers — it has the confidence a balance figure needs. Inter runs the UI and body copy, set with tabular figures wherever amounts line up. Instrument Serif Italic — a serif, not a sans, which is why it needed to actually look different up close — is rationed to one job: a single insight line, never a control.
